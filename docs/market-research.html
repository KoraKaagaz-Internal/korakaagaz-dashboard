Invoicing tools: what's out there, and how we beat it

We compared 15 invoicing tools that Indian freelancers and studios use. Global tools (FreshBooks, Bonsai, Xero, Wave, Stripe) look polished but don't handle GST, e-invoicing or UPI. Indian tools (Zoho Invoice, Refrens, Swipe, myBillBook, Vyapar) are compliant, but they're built for traders with inventory, and they charge per user or per message for the useful parts: WhatsApp, reminders, team roles.

Our angle: a private, invite-only tool shaped around how a design studio works. Quote, advance, invoice, part payments with TDS, credit notes, exports under LUT. It's GST-correct and keeps the Kora Kaagaz look. Running cost: ₹0 (Vercel Hobby, Supabase free, Gmail SMTP, UPI QR, wa.me links).

Prices change often and many come from review sites (Capterra, GetApp). Treat them as indicative. Anything not confirmed from a primary source is marked “unverified”.

Swipe deep-dive: what we took, what we left

Swipe is the most feature-dense Indian tool. Its help forum works as its knowledge base, and the Play Store shows 4.7★ from about 15.5k reviews. Reviewers praise its speed and complain about bot-only support.

Taken (free to run)

  • Document series per type and financial year (QT/26-27/…, CN/26-27/…).
  • Quote → invoice/proforma in one click; won/lost tracking with a reason.
  • Customer ledger with a date-range statement.
  • Outstanding-balance reminder per client.
  • Terms library per document type, snapshotted into each document.
  • Multiple bank accounts chosen per document (INR account + SWIFT account).
  • Editable WhatsApp and email message templates.
  • Signature/stamp image; round-off toggle.
  • Custom fields (PO No., project code).
  • Projects with budget.
  • FX rate fetch on export invoices.
  • Keyboard shortcuts.
  • Approval workflow.
  • PIN → state.

Left out, and why

  • Inventory, warehouses, barcodes, POS, thermal print, e-way bill: goods only.
  • Automated WhatsApp/SMS, AI fill, DSC signing, payment links: they cost money per use.
  • Tally/Shopify sync: not needed yet; our CSVs cover the CA hand-off.

India compliance notes for a design studio

GST invoice (CGST Rule 46)

  • Supplier: name, address and GSTIN.
  • Invoice number: serial, consecutive, max 16 characters, letters/digits/-// only, unique per financial year.
  • Date.
  • Recipient: name, address and GSTIN if registered.
  • Per line: HSN/SAC, description, taxable value after discount, rate and amount of each tax.
  • Place of supply: state name and code.
  • Reverse charge: whether it applies.
  • Signature: required, but not on electronic invoices.
  • Design services: usually SAC 998391 at 18%. Four-digit SAC is enough up to ₹5 Cr turnover.

Documents

  • Receipt voucher for advances (Rule 50).
  • Credit notes for corrections (Section 34). Issue them by 30 November after the year ends.
  • Amount in words: not legally required, but expected.

E-invoicing (IRN)

Mandatory only above ₹5 Cr aggregate turnover. From April 2025, taxpayers above ₹10 Cr must report within 30 days. Not needed for us now.

Exports of services

Zero-rated. File an LUT every financial year to invoice without IGST, and print “SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT UNDER LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX”. Keep the FIRC or bank advice. Whether INR receipts qualify: check with your CA.

TDS

Clients usually deduct 10% under s.194J (professional services) above ₹50,000 a year. It may be 2% (technical) or 1–2% under s.194C depending on how the client classifies the work. Ask your CA. From 1 April 2026 the same rates sit under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Track TDS per payment and match it with Form 26AS.

Agency tools: running the whole studio, not just billing

The goal moved from "invoice generator" to one tool for the whole studio: projects and tasks, time and shoots, leads, documents with e-signatures, client onboarding, a client portal and proofing. Invoicing is one part of it. We looked at 40+ tools in three groups:

  • Agency project management: Productive, Teamwork, Scoro, Accelo, Function Point, Workamajig, Kantata, ClickUp, Monday, Asana, Notion, Zoho, Odoo, and open source (Plane, OpenProject, Leantime, Huly, Twenty).
  • Client-facing tools: HoneyBook, Dubsado, Bonsai, Moxie, Plutio, Clientjoy, Assembly (formerly Copilot), ManyRequests, SuperOkay, Clientary, Agency Handy, Wayfront (formerly SPP), Content Snare, plus proofing tools Filestage, Ziflow and Pastel, and CRMs Bigin, HubSpot and Pipedrive.
  • Documents and e-sign: PandaDoc, Proposify, Better Proposals, Qwilr, DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, Zoho Sign, Documenso, DocuSeal, OpenSign, Leegality, Digio, emSigner.

Where the market leaves room

  1. Nobody joins Indian billing to project management. Agency tools don't do GST, TDS or UPI. Indian billing tools don't do projects.
  2. Shoot, crew and kit booking is unserved. Tools offer generic calendars; only Odoo Enterprise has real resource planning.
  3. Proofing is expensive. Filestage and Ziflow start around $199 a month; most project tools don't have it at all.
  4. Portals, white-label and custom domains sit on upper tiers. Most tools charge per seat with minimums (Monday 3, Scoro 5, Teamwork 3–5), so freelancers and clients cost money.
  5. The common complaints are learnable: setup overload (Scoro, Dubsado), clients forgetting portal passwords, weak mobile apps, and automation quotas on free tiers (ClickUp 100/month, Zoho 50).

E-signatures and documents in India (not legal advice)

  • Click-to-sign is a valid contract, with weaker evidence. Section 10A of the IT Act says a contract isn't unenforceable just because it was made electronically. But a typed or drawn signature isn't a Second Schedule technique, so it doesn't get the statutory presumption that Aadhaar eSign or a DSC gets. We have to prove who signed. Hence: email OTP, IP and time, a document hash, and a certificate page.
  • Never e-sign First Schedule documents: powers of attorney (with narrow exceptions), wills, trusts, and negotiable instruments other than cheques. None of a studio's usual paperwork falls here.
  • Copyright licences and assignments must be in writing and signed (ss.19, 30). If term and territory aren't stated, they default to 5 years and India. So our usage-rights certificate makes term, territory and media mandatory.
  • Stamp duty is set by each state. Since the Supreme Court's 7-judge ruling (Dec 2023), an unstamped agreement is inadmissible until stamped, not void. For MSAs and long retainers, buy a state e-stamp and attach it as page 1.
  • Evidence: Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (in force 1 July 2024) asks for a certificate with a hash value for electronic records. Our audit trail stores the SHA-256 for this reason.
  • Model releases need a guardian signature for minors and should cover consent to use personal data (DPDP Act 2023).

Files at ₹0

Supabase free storage is 1 GB with a 50 MB per-file limit, and Vercel caps request bodies at 4.5 MB. So browsers upload straight to storage, big photos are shrunk to WebP, and raw shoots and PSD/AI files stay on Google Drive as links. Cloudflare R2's free 10 GB would help, but it needs a card on file, so we skipped it.
