Kora Kaagaz Studio

Live: app.korakaagaz.co (also invoice-generator-flax-pi.vercel.app) · Supabase project invoice-generator (ref komdaeylldwknhwcjevv, Mumbai) · Vercel project invoice-generator · Market research: market-research.html

Where it's going: one tool for the whole studio. Live so far: invoicing; projects and tasks; time, team and calendar; expenses with GST credit and TDS; analytics; and the CRM. Next, in order: documents with e-signatures; client onboarding and a client portal; proofing; then a studio dashboard with automations. The plan and research are in market-research.html.

Projects (live): start a project from a studio template (brand identity, product shoot, campaign shoot, social retainer, packaging, website). Then work it on a board, list or timeline, with a task side panel for assignees, dates, estimates, checklist, comments with @mentions and files. Each project also has deliverables with revision limits, a team, a staff-only budget, a billing tab and an activity feed. "My work" lists everyone's tasks, and a bell shows notifications. Freelancers see only the projects they're added to and never see clients or money.

CRM (live):

  • Leads board with stages Enquiry → Discovery call → Proposal sent → Negotiation → Won / Lost. Lost asks for a reason, and each lead remembers how far it got.
  • Each lead has a page with a history of calls, WhatsApp, email and notes, a next follow-up date, and buttons to email, WhatsApp or call.
  • Won → set up creates the client (or links one with the same email) and a project from a template. A quote is then one click away.
  • Enquiry form at /enquire: public, embeddable with ?embed=1, and tags the source with ?source=instagram. Spam is caught with a hidden field, a 3-second minimum, and 5 per hour from one hashed address.
  • Booking page at /book/<slug>: free times come from working hours minus calendar events (with a buffer). A booking creates a calendar meeting, moves the lead to Discovery call, and emails an .ics invite.
  • Follow-ups show on Home and as daily notifications. Reports gains a pipeline section: lead funnel, sources, and open pipeline by stage.

Expenses (live):

  • Bills, receipts and team claims. Each records the vendor (GSTIN, PAN, TDS type), category, receipt photo or PDF, project and payment.
  • GST. The split (CGST/SGST vs IGST) is automatic. Input credit rules follow the category, for example meals are blocked under s.17(5). Reverse charge is supported.
  • TDS. The app suggests TDS from each vendor's running total for the financial year.
  • Claims and bills. Claims go through admin approval and are reimbursed in a batch. Bills to pay have due dates and an MSME 45-day flag.
  • Re-billing and recurring costs. Billable expenses can be re-billed to the client with a markup. Recurring rent and subscriptions are drafted by the daily job.
  • GST & TDS page. Input credit in GSTR-3B Table 4 form, GSTR-2B matching from the portal's CSV, TDS deposits, and a purchase register plus TDS CSVs. Filing due dates appear on Home and Reports.

Reports (rebuilt): period, comparison, basis and client filters, then eight headline numbers (revenue, gross income, profit, cash and runway, money owed and days to get paid, utilisation, biggest-client share, quote win rate), then:

  • Profit & loss: monthly bars, a waterfall, margin trend, spend by category, budget vs actual.
  • Clients & services: top clients against a 20% risk line, revenue by service, new vs returning, retention cohorts, client value.
  • Cash & collections: billed vs collected, days-to-get-paid trend against 45 days, a 13-week cash forecast, ageing, TDS both ways.
  • Projects & team: project profit (revenue − expenses) with hours against budget, a team-hours heatmap (hours logged ÷ available), on-time %, the quote funnel.

Every chart has a tooltip, a legend and a "View as table" option.

Speed: pages load in about 0.2–0.5 s, down from 2–3.5 s. Functions now run in Mumbai next to the database, and the login check runs once per page.

Time, team and calendar (live):

  • Time: a timer (from a task or the Time page; it shows in the header) and a weekly timesheet. Approval is optional (Settings → Timesheets, off by default): when on, people submit their week and the admin approves it; when off, hours count as soon as they're logged.
  • Project Time tab: hours against budget, this month's retainer use, and hours by person. No money: the studio never bills or costs by the hour.
  • Calendar: month, week, and crew & kit views for shoots, meetings and deadlines. You book team, outside crew and kit, double bookings are flagged, and each shoot has a printable call sheet. Everyone gets a private feed for Google Calendar.
  • People: leave requests with admin approval, studio holidays, and a 6-week workload view of booked hours against capacity.

The billing side is a private, invite-only invoicing workspace. It handles quotes, proforma invoices, tax invoices, receipt vouchers (advances) and credit notes in the Kora Kaagaz design, GST-compliant (Rule 46), with UPI QR codes, client share links, email/WhatsApp sending, reminders, part payments with TDS, projects, a client ledger, a dashboard and GSTR-1 CSV exports. Running cost: ₹0.

  • Stack: Next.js 15.5.28 (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind 4) on Vercel Hobby · Supabase free (Postgres with RLS, Google auth, Storage) · Puppeteer + @sparticuz/chromium for PDFs · Gmail SMTP (nodemailer) for email · Vercel Cron for the daily job.
  • Access: Google sign-in. team@korakaagaz.co is the only admin; the admin invites Members, Viewers and Freelancers. A database trigger refuses to create an account for any email that isn't invited, so people who find the link can't get in.
  • Template: one fixed 852 × 1278 px React template (DocumentTemplate) renders the editor preview, the client share page, the print page and the PDF, so they always match.
Setup left for you: a Gmail app password for team@korakaagaz.co so emails actually send (until then they're logged as "skipped" and invites show a message you can copy); Google sign-in and app.korakaagaz.co are live (2026-10-31).

    Secrets

    Kept in .env.local (git-ignored) and Vercel env vars only: Supabase service-role key (used by the daily cron only), CRON_SECRET, Gmail app password (SMTP_PASS), DB password. The anon key is public by design; RLS protects the data.

    Calendar feed

    /api/ics/<token>.ics is public but keyed by a random 128-bit token per person (members.ics_token), like a share link. It uses the service-role client to return only that person's bookings, assigned task due dates, approved leave and holidays. "Make a new link" rotates the token, which breaks the old URL.

    Share links

    Each document has a random 128-bit token. /d/<token> calls a security definer function that returns only that document (never drafts) and counts views. A link can be turned off or replaced. Pages are noindex.

    Money rules

    subtotal → discount (flat/%) → taxable → GST (CGST+SGST if place of supply = our state, IGST otherwise, none for exports under LUT) → optional round-off → total. Balance = total − payments − TDS − issued credit notes. TDS shortcuts apply to the pre-GST share of the balance. All maths lives in src/lib/money.ts, shared by the preview and the server.

    Glossary

    Issue
    Turning a draft into a real document: it gets the next number in its series and becomes locked. Changes after that go through a credit note or cancellation, as GST requires.
    Place of supply
    The state where the client is (or "Outside India"). It decides IGST (different state) vs CGST+SGST (same state).
    SAC
    Services Accounting Code, the services version of HSN. Design work is usually 998391.
    LUT
    Letter of Undertaking, filed each financial year so exports can be invoiced without IGST.
    Receipt voucher
    The GST document for an advance payment (Rule 50); later adjusted against the final invoice.
    Credit note
    Reduces or reverses an issued invoice (Section 34).
    TDS
    Tax a business client deducts before paying you (usually 10% under s.194J). You claim it back; match it with Form 26AS.
    GSTR-1
    The monthly/quarterly return of outward supplies. Our CSVs follow the GST offline tool's columns.
    RLS
    Row-level security: database rules that decide who can read or write each row.
    Share link
    /d/<token>: a private, unguessable link that shows one document to a client without logging in.
    Audit log
    An append-only record of who created, edited, issued, paid or cancelled what.
